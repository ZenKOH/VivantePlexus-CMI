# Product roadmap

## Generation 0 — CMI Instrument
Canonical data model, protocol engine, timing quality, simulator and research export.

**Exit criterion:** complete session reconstruction and deterministic analytics.

## Generation 1 — CMI Assess
Standardised single-task and dual-task assessment, descriptive profiles, capacity mapping and longitudinal comparison.

**Exit criterion:** acceptable bench and test-retest reliability.

## Generation 2 — CMI Train
Transparent deterministic adaptation around an observed capacity boundary.

**Exit criterion:** adaptive rules remain interpretable and do not produce unsafe or unstable challenge escalation.

## Generation 3 — CMI Connect
Hardware-independent device SDK and adapters for cycling, FES, gait, balance, wearables and rehabilitation robotics.

**Exit criterion:** a new device can integrate without modifying protocol or analytics layers.

## Generation 4 — CMI Evidence
Research-mode protocol locking, multisite provenance, validation datasets and external studies.

**Exit criterion:** reliability, validity and responsiveness evidence supports a clearly defined intended use.

## Generation 5 — CMI Intelligence
Prediction, clustering and decision support only when high-quality longitudinal data and external validation justify them.

## Explicit kill criteria

Redesign or stop if:

1. repeated measurements are too unstable to distinguish change from noise;
2. practice effects cannot be controlled;
3. sensor/platform latency prevents trustworthy event alignment;
4. clinicians cannot interpret reports consistently;
5. capacity maps are dominated by arbitrary task choice;
6. hardware independence turns into bespoke integration work for every device;
7. users systematically reprioritise despite controlled instructions;
8. customers will pay for entertainment but not measurement/reporting;
9. improvement fails to transfer beyond trained tasks;
10. desired clinical claims create regulatory/evidence costs that invalidate the business case.
